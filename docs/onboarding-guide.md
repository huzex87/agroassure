# AgroAssure: getting started

Pick your role. Each takes a few minutes.

## Administrators

1. **Sign in.** Open the console, enter your work email, and click **Email me a sign-in link**. Open the email and click the link.
2. **Add facilities.** *Facilities → Add facility*, or *Import* your spreadsheet (CSV).
3. **Check the checklists.** *Settings → Checklists*. Each facility type needs one in force.
4. **Add your people.**
   - Inspectors: *Settings → Team → Invite an inspector*. Enter a name and phone number.
   - Office staff: *Settings → Team → Add a colleague*. Enter a name, email and role.
5. **Plan visits.** *Visits*: choose an inspector, tick the facilities, then **Plan visit**.

## Inspectors

1. **Install the app** from the link in your invite message.
2. **Tap the link** in the message, or type the code (like `K7PM-4XQ2`) and tap **Continue**.
3. **Do the visit.** Tap a visit, answer **Yes**, **No** or **N/A**, and add a short note for every **No**. Then **Finish and sign**, have the facility representative sign, and **Submit**.

Your work sends by itself when there is a signal. A PIN is optional: *Account → App PIN*.

## Office staff

1. **Open the email** that says you've been added, click **Sign in to AgroAssure**, then **Email me a sign-in link**, then the link in the new email.
2. **Review.** *Inspections*: open one, then **Record a decision**. *Issues to fix* lists what needs follow-up.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No code arrived, or it expired (they last 3 days) | Administrator: *Settings → Team → Send new code* |
| Sign-in link expired (they last 15 minutes, once) | Ask for a new one on the sign-in page |
| Lost or borrowed phone | Administrator: *Settings → Team → Phones → Sign out this phone* |
| Forgot the app PIN | Tap **Forgot PIN?**, then ask your administrator for a new code. Unsent work is kept. |
