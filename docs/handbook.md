# AgroAssure handbook

How to run an inspection programme on AgroAssure. Find your role and follow it from the top.

**Console address:** the website your administrator gave you. Sign in there with your work email.

## How it works

1. **Plan a visit.** An administrator or reviewer picks an inspector and the facilities to visit.
2. **Inspect on the phone.** The inspector answers the checklist on site, adds notes and photos, and has the facility representative sign. No signal is needed while working.
3. **Review the result.** Office staff open the inspection in the console, check it, and record a decision.
4. **Authorise the certificate.** The authorising officer who made the decision authorises it once every finding is verified closed.

## Administrators

**The first week.** Tick each as you go.

1. **Sign in.** Open the console, enter your work email, click **Email me a sign-in link**, then open the link in your inbox. It works once and expires after 15 minutes.
2. **Check the system.** *Settings → System status*. Fix anything marked red, then click **Send me a test email** to prove email works.
3. **Add your facilities.** *Facilities → Add facility*, or *Import* the spreadsheet you already keep.
4. **Check the checklists.** *Settings → Checklists*. Each type of facility needs one in force.
5. **Invite your inspectors.** *Settings → Team → Invite an inspector*. Enter a name and a phone number or email. They receive a code that lasts 3 days; use **Send new code** if it runs out.
6. **Add your office staff.** *Settings → Team → Add a colleague*. Enter a name, work email and role. They get a welcome email with their sign-in link.
7. **Plan the first visits.** *Visits*: choose an inspector, tick the facilities, then **Plan visit**.

**Every week**

- Open *Home*. The sentence at the top says what needs attention today.
- Check *Risk-targeted inspections* for facilities worth visiting next, each with the reason it was suggested.
- Plan the week's visits under *Visits*.
- Open *Findings* and chase anything past its due date.

**A lost phone.** *Settings → Team → Phones → Sign out this phone*. It stops working at once. What it already sent stays on the record. Then use **Send new code** to set up a new phone.

## Inspectors

You need the AgroAssure app and the invite message your administrator sent.

**First time**

1. Install the app from the link in your invite message.
2. Tap the link in the message, or open the app and type the code (like `K7PM-4XQ2`) and tap **Continue**.
3. You are in. A PIN is optional: *Account → App PIN*.

**Each visit**

1. Open *Today* and tap your visit.
2. Answer each question **Yes**, **No** or **N/A**.
3. For every **No**, write a short note. Add a photo where it helps. Photos cannot be changed after you submit.
4. Tap **Finish and sign**. The facility representative signs with a finger.
5. Tap **Submit**.

You can work with no signal. Finished work sends by itself when the phone finds a connection.

## Office staff

**Getting in.** Open the welcome email and click **Sign in to AgroAssure**, then **Email me a sign-in link**, then the link in the new email.

**Reviewing an inspection**

1. Open *Inspections* and choose one. Answers, findings and evidence are on the left.
2. On the right, pick a decision under *Record a decision*, add the basis if useful, and click **Record decision**. A change of mind is a new decision; the record is never overwritten.
3. When a facility says a finding is fixed, check it and click **Verify closed**.
4. The authorising officer who made the decision clicks **Authorise certificate** once every finding is verified closed. It is recorded against their name and cannot be taken back, so the console asks them to confirm first.

## Handy things in the console

- **Find anything fast.** Press **Ctrl+K** (or click **Search** at the top of the sidebar) and type a facility name, a licence number or a page name.
- **Download the registry.** On *Facilities*, click **Export**. You get a spreadsheet of what the page shows for your current search and filter. Long lists show 50 at a time, with **Next** and **Previous** underneath.
- **Dark mode.** Click **Dark mode** at the bottom of the sidebar. The console otherwise follows your device, and remembers your choice on that browser.
- **On a phone or tablet.** The sections become a row of buttons across the top of the page. Everything works, but the review pages are easiest on a larger screen.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No code arrived, or it expired | Administrator: *Settings → Team → Send new code* |
| Sign-in link expired or already used | Ask for a new one on the sign-in page. Links last 15 minutes and work once |
| Sign-in page says "Getting things ready" | The service was resting. Wait up to a minute; the page continues by itself |
| Nobody can sign in | Check the console address, and that the person was added under *Settings → Team*. Only people an administrator added can sign in |
| Emails are not arriving | Administrator: *Settings → System status*, then **Send me a test email** |
| Lost or borrowed phone | Administrator: *Settings → Team → Phones → Sign out this phone* |
| Forgot the app PIN | Tap **Forgot PIN?**, then ask your administrator for a new code |
| Work has not appeared in the console | The phone sends when it has a signal. Open the app with the phone online and wait a minute |

## Words used

- **Facility:** a business that is inspected, such as an agro-dealer or a blending plant.
- **Visit:** one planned trip by an inspector to one facility.
- **Inspection:** the completed checklist from a visit, with answers, notes, photos and a signature.
- **Finding:** a problem found on a visit. The console lists them under *Findings* with a due date.
- **Decision:** the outcome an officer records after reviewing an inspection.
- **Certificate:** the record of compliance. AgroAssure prepares and shows it for the mandated regulator; the regulator issues it.
- **Roles:** *Administrator* manages the team and settings and plans visits. *Reviewer* plans visits and records decisions. *Authorising officer* also authorises certificates. *Auditor* can only read.
