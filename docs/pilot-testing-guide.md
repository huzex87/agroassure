Thank you for helping test AgroAssure before it goes into daily use. This guide
tells you what to try, what should happen, and how to tell us when it doesn't.

## What AgroAssure does, in one minute

AgroAssure records inspections of fertilizer and agro-input businesses:
agro-dealer warehouses, blending plants, manufacturers and importers.

- **Inspectors** use the **phone app** on site. It works **without internet**:
  answers, photos and signatures are saved on the phone and send themselves
  when there is a signal.
- **Office staff** (supervisors, authorising officers, administrators) use the
  **console**, a website. They plan visits, review inspections, follow up
  issues and manage the team.

Every inspection is signed by the phone it was done on, so the record always
shows who did it, where and when. Nothing can be quietly changed afterwards.

---

## Getting started in 60 seconds

You don't set anything up yourself. Your administrator adds you, and a message
arrives.

| You are | What arrives | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| An **inspector** | An **SMS** (and email) with a link and a code like `K7PM-4XQ2` | Install the app, tap the link in the message, tap **Continue**. Your visits appear by themselves. |
| **Office staff** | An **email** saying you've been added | Open the console, type your work email, click the link that arrives. |

That's all. There's no password, no language question (the app uses your
phone's language), and no PIN to choose before you can start. If you didn't
get a message, ask your supervisor to send it again.

## A. Inspectors

### A1. Install the app

1. On your Android phone, open the **app download link** (at the top of this
   guide, or in your invite message).
2. Download the file. If Android asks, allow **"Install unknown apps"** for
   your browser, then tap **Install**.
3. Open **AgroAssure**.

- [ ] *Expected:* it opens straight to a code box, in your phone's language
      (Hausa if your phone is set to Hausa, otherwise English). Tap **Hausa**
      or **English** at the top right to switch. You can also change it later
      under **Account**.

### A2. Get into the app

You were sent an invite code (by SMS or email, like `K7PM-4XQ2`).

1. Type the code into the box, or just tap the link in the message.
2. Tap **Continue**.

- [ ] *Expected:* **"Welcome, [your name]"**, then **See today's visits**.
      Nothing asks you for a PIN first.
- [ ] *Expected:* under the code box it says **"No code? Ask your supervisor to
      invite you."** There is no other way in, unless your administrator has
      switched on self-registration (see below).

**Adding a PIN (optional).** Under **See today's visits** there's a quieter
**Add a PIN** button. A PIN is an extra lock for phones that are shared or get
borrowed. You can skip it now and add one later: **Account → App PIN → Set a
PIN**.

- [ ] *Try this:* tap **Add a PIN**, type one, then type a *different* one to
      confirm. *Expected:* "Those didn't match", and you start again.

**Only if registration is switched on: you have no code, so you register
yourself.** You'll only see **Register instead** on the first screen if your
administrator turned this on. Most pilots leave it off.

1. On the first screen, tap **Register instead**.
2. Fill in your **full name, phone number, email** and **state**. Tap
   **Send my codes**.
3. You'll get **two 6-digit codes**: one by **SMS**, one by **email**. Type both
   and tap **Confirm**.
   - [ ] *Try this:* type one code wrong on purpose. *Expected:* it tells you
         **which** code is wrong, and keeps the one you got right.
4. You'll see **"Waiting for approval"**. You can close the app. Your
   administrator has been told.
5. When they approve you, you'll get an SMS. Open the app.

- [ ] *Expected:* it goes straight to **"Welcome"**, with **no invite code and
      no PIN needed**.
- [ ] If you were **not** approved, it shows the reason your administrator gave.

### A3. Do an inspection

1. On **Today's visits**, you'll see the facilities you've been assigned. It
   may take a minute after first opening the app. Tap one.
2. Answer each question **Yes**, **No** or **N/A**.
   - A **No** needs a short note of what you saw.
   - Tap **Add photo** to capture evidence. Photos can't be changed after you
     submit.
3. When every question is answered, tap **Finish and sign**.
4. Sign yourself, then hand the phone to the **facility representative** to
   type their name and role and sign.
5. Tap **Submit inspection**.

- [ ] *Expected:* **"Inspection submitted"**. It is saved on the phone and
      sends by itself.

### A4. Try these as well

- [ ] **No signal.** Turn on **Airplane mode**, do a whole inspection, and
      submit it. *Expected:* it still works, and it says the work will send when
      there's a signal. Turn Airplane mode off. Within a few minutes, **Account**
      should say **"Everything is sent"**, or tap **Send now**.
- [ ] **Standing away from the facility.** Start an inspection somewhere other
      than the facility's address. *Expected:* a calm note that you seem to be
      some distance away. It's recorded for your supervisor, and you can carry
      on.
- [ ] **PIN.** Add a PIN first (**Account → App PIN → Set a PIN**). Then close
      the app, leave it for 5+ minutes, and reopen it. *Expected:* it asks for
      your PIN. Enter a wrong PIN: it tells you how many tries are left.
- [ ] **Forgot PIN.** Tap **Forgot PIN?** (only if you're happy to be sent a
      new code). *Expected:* your unsent work is kept and sends once you're back
      in with a new code.
- [ ] **Language.** Switch between English and Hausa under **Account**. Tell us
      about any Hausa wording that reads wrong or unnatural. This matters a lot.

## B. Office staff

### B1. Get into the console

Your administrator added you with your work email.

1. Open the **console address** (at the top of this guide).
2. Enter your **work email** and click **Email me a sign-in link**.
3. Open the email and click the link, then click **Continue**.

- [ ] *Expected:* you land on the console. There is no password, ever.
- [ ] *Expected:* the sign-in page is just the email box. There's no **Request
      access** link, unless your administrator switched on self-registration.

**Only if registration is switched on: you ask to join.** You'll only see
**New here? Request access** if your administrator turned this on.

1. Open the console address and click **New here? Request access**.
2. Fill in your name, work email, phone and state. Click **Continue**.
3. Enter the **code from your email** and the **code from your SMS**. Click
   **Confirm**.
4. You'll see **"Waiting for approval"**. You can close the page. You'll get an
   email and SMS when an administrator decides.
5. Once approved, go back to the console and sign in with your email, as in
   Way 1.

- [ ] *Expected:* after approval, the request page says **"Welcome aboard"**
      and names the role you were given.

> Sign-in links work **once** and expire after **15 minutes**. If one fails,
> ask for a new one. Your email's security scanner can't use it up, because of
> the **Continue** button.

### B2. Look around

You'll only see the pages your role can use.

- [ ] **Dashboard.** A summary of facilities, inspections, open issues and
      certificates.
- [ ] **Facilities.** Search for a facility and open it.
- [ ] **Inspections.** Open an inspection an inspector has submitted. Check you
      can see every answer, the notes, photos, the check-in location and both
      signatures.
- [ ] **Issues to fix.** Every "No" answer becomes an issue with a due date.
      Check they make sense.

### B3. If you plan visits (supervisors)

1. Open **Plan visits**.
2. Choose an inspector and one or more facilities, and a date. Save.

- [ ] *Expected:* within a few minutes, the visits appear on the inspector's
      phone under **Today's visits** (they may need a signal).

### B4. If you decide outcomes (authorising officers)

1. Open a submitted inspection and use **Record a decision**.

- [ ] *Expected:* the decision is added to the record. Decisions are never
      edited; a change of mind is recorded as a new decision.
- [ ] If a certificate is issued, open it and check it names **you** as the
      authorising officer, and that its QR code opens the public check page.

## C. Administrators

Do section B first, then these.

### C1. Approve people who asked to join

*Skip this section unless you switched on self-registration
(`SELF_REGISTRATION=on`). By default nobody can ask to join, so there is nothing
to approve: you invite people directly (section C2).*

1. When someone registers, you get an **email**, and the **Dashboard** shows
   **"N people are waiting to join"**. Click **Review**, or open **Team**.
2. Each request shows their name, email and phone. A **green tick** means they
   proved it's theirs with a code, and it shows where they registered (phone
   app or website).
3. Choose their **Role** and click **Approve**, or click **Reject** and give a
   reason (they'll receive it).

- [ ] Approve an inspector who registered **in the app**. *Expected:* their
      phone works straight away, with no code, and it appears under
      **Team → Phones**.
- [ ] Approve an inspector who registered **on the website**. *Expected:* they
      automatically get an invite code by SMS and email to set up the app.
- [ ] Approve someone as **Desk supervisor**. *Expected:* they can sign in to
      the console with their email.
- [ ] Reject one with a reason. *Expected:* they're told, with your reason.
- [ ] Only you should see requests **for your state**. A national
      administrator sees all states.

### C2. Invite someone directly

This is the normal way people join.

1. **Team → Invite an inspector.** Enter a name and phone number (and email if
   you have it).

- [ ] *Expected:* the screen shows the code and says whether the **SMS** and
      **email** were sent. If you're standing next to them, they can type the
      code you see.
- [ ] **Team → Add a colleague** for office staff. *Expected:* they can sign in
      with that email straight away.

### C3. Set up your state

- [ ] **Facilities → Add facility → Import.** Upload your spreadsheet (CSV).
      *Expected:* a preview first, then import. Duplicate licence numbers are
      flagged, not imported twice.
- [ ] **Checklists.** Make sure each facility type you inspect has a checklist
      **in force**. Without one, an inspector can't start that inspection.
- [ ] Follow the **"Set up your state in five steps"** list on the Dashboard.
      It ticks itself as you go.

### C4. A lost phone

1. **Team → Phones**. Next to a test phone, type a reason (e.g. "lost") and
   click **Sign out this phone**.

- [ ] *Expected:* the phone stops working on its next connection and shows
      **"This phone was signed out"**. Work it already sent stays on record.
- [ ] Send that person a **new code**. *Expected:* they're back in, and the
      phone appears as a new active phone.

## How to report a problem

When something doesn't match the *Expected* line, or confuses you, send us a
message with:

1. **Who you are and your role**, e.g. "Aisha, inspector, Katsina".
2. **Where**: phone app or console, and which screen.
3. **What you did**, step by step.
4. **What you expected**, and **what actually happened**. Copy any message
   **word for word**.
5. **A screenshot** if you can. On most Android phones, press Power +
   Volume down together.
6. **When** it happened (date and roughly what time) and whether you had a
   signal.

Also tell us about anything **slow, unclear, or badly worded**, in English or
Hausa. That's as useful as a bug.

## Common questions

**Do I need internet to inspect?**
No. Only to receive your visits the first time and to send finished work. Both
happen by themselves when there's a signal.

**I didn't get my code.**
Check your spam folder for the email. For SMS, wait a minute, then ask your
administrator to **Send new code**. If your number is on the do-not-disturb
list, tell us.

**My code "has expired".**
Invite codes last **3 days** (registration codes, where registration is on,
last 30 minutes). Ask your administrator for a new one.

**"Too many requests to join from here."** *(only where registration is on)*
The same phone number or email can only ask 3 times an hour. Wait an hour, or
ask your administrator to invite you instead.

**"An account with this email already exists."**
You're already on the team. Sign in instead, or ask your administrator to send
a code for the phone app.

**Do I have to choose a PIN?**
No. It's optional. Add one any time from **Account → App PIN** if the phone is
shared or might be borrowed.

**Can I use AgroAssure on an iPhone?**
Not in this pilot. The test app is Android only.

**Is my data safe if I lose the phone?**
Tell your administrator straight away: they can sign the phone out, and it
stops working on its next connection. Anything it already sent stays safely on
record. If you added an app PIN, the app was also locked behind it.
